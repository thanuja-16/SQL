Equi join:
to join the two or more tables using = operator
select col1,col2 from tablename join tablename
on col1=col1

select e.*,d.* from employee e join department d
on e.dept_id=d.dept_id;

Non Equi join:
to join the one or more tables using other than = operator such as using <,> and between etc.

select e.emp_name,d.dept_name,e.salary from employee e
    -> join department d on e.dept_id=d.dept_id
    -> where e.salary>50000;

select e.*,d.dept_name from employee e
    -> join department d on e.dept_id=d.dept_id
    -> where e.salary between 45000 and 70000;
